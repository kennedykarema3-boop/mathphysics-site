Projectile Motion Simulation

Change the launch speed and angle, press Launch, and compare the path with the formulas.

The formulas behind it

Time of flight: T = 2u sin θ / g
Maximum height: H = u² sin²θ / 2g
Range: R = u² sin 2θ / g
Try this: launch at 30° and then at 60° with the same speed. What do you notice about the range? Which angle gives the longest range?
